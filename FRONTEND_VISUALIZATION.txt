KEY:
Cg = CHARGE FROM GRID (purple)    Buy = BUY FROM GRID (red)
Store = STORE SOLAR (green)       Disc = DISCHARGE BATTERY (blue)
Sell = SELL TO GRID (green)

COLOR CODING:
🟢 Green   = Profit/Storage operations (SELL, STORE)
🔴 Red     = Forced purchases (BUY)
🟡 Yellow  = Default/Neutral
🔵 Blue    = Battery discharge (cost avoidance)
🟣 Purple  = Strategic charging (investment)
